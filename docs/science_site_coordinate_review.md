# Scientific-site coordinate verification (Jezero Pathfinder)

Three site positions are sourced from the peer-reviewed Jezero crater-floor
sample table and were previously tested in this project:

| Site | Latitude (N) | Longitude (E) | App status |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Rochette | 18.43074 | 77.44437 | Route selectable, subject to live raster check |
| Brac | 18.43397 | 77.44301 | Route selectable, subject to live raster check |
| Roubion | 18.42769 | 77.45165 | Route selectable, subject to live raster check |

Source: Simon et al. (2023), *Samples Collected From the Floor of
Jezero Crater With the Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover*.
https://doi.org/10.1029/2022JE007474

The remaining three are *scientifically documented* but do not yet have
verified precise target coordinates for this application:

| Site | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Wildcat Ridge | https://science.nasa.gov/resource/two-perseverance-sampling-locations-in-jezeros-delta/ | Exact target coordinate pending |
| Cheyava Falls | https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-perseverance-rover-scientists-find-intriguing-mars-rock/ | Exact target coordinate pending |
| Kodiak Outcrop | https://doi.org/10.1029/2023JE008205 | Target coordinate pending; a butte, not a sampled rover waypoint |

Do not infer a particular rock's coordinates from a broad mission map, a rover
position at a different sol or a generic crater coordinate. The app intentionally
retains these targets as information-only until coordinates and hazard-cell
eligibility are checked. Use the independent point-to-point planner for
prototype exploratory routing between manually chosen points. A green check for
routability does not imply crewed-vehicle safety.
